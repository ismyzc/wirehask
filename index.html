Features

Everything you need to understand your network

From a quick DNS check to a multi-gigabyte incident investigation, Netrova gives you complete visibility at every layer.

Live Packet Capture

Capture from Ethernet, Wi-Fi, loopback, USB and VPN interfaces with ring buffers, auto-rotation and zero-drop buffering at line rate.

Deep Protocol Dissection

Over 1,800 dissectors decode every field of every layer — from Ethernet frames to application payloads — in a navigable tree.

Powerful Display Filters

An expressive filter language with autocomplete and syntax checking. Isolate exactly the traffic you care about in milliseconds.

TLS & QUIC Decryption

Decrypt TLS 1.2/1.3 and QUIC sessions with key-log files or RSA keys to inspect HTTPS, HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 traffic in clear text.

Statistics & Graphs

I/O graphs, conversation and endpoint tables, protocol hierarchy, flow diagrams and TCP stream graphs — all exportable.

Expert Diagnostics

Automatic detection of retransmissions, zero windows, out-of-order segments, malformed packets and latency spikes.

Stream Reassembly

Follow any conversation from first byte to last

Reassemble TCP, UDP, TLS, HTTP/2 and QUIC streams into a readable, color-coded dialogue between client and server. Export payloads, extract transferred files and search inside streams.

  • Follow TCP, UDP, TLS, HTTP and QUIC streams
  • Export objects from HTTP, SMB, FTP and IMF
  • ASCII, hex, C array, raw and YAML views
